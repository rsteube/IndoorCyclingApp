Indoor Bike 2.11

Start a long route wherever you like along it.

Added
- **Start a long route anywhere along it.** A route of 20 km or more now opens on its whole map and elevation profile before the ride. Tap the map to start at the nearest point of the road, or tap or drag along the profile to put the start exactly where you want it: the foot of the climb, the second half, the place you stopped last time (offered as a button, and picked for you). The part before your start is dimmed, and the distance and climbing left are shown. The ride then starts there like any other, with the climbing behind you counted as done. Shorter routes start at the beginning as before.

Fixed
- **A route map that opened showing the whole route could freeze the app.** The map tried to fit the route before it had a size on screen. It now waits until it has one.

Notes
- On the iPhone and Sailfish OS apps, routes still start from the beginning or from where you stopped.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices, connections and settings carry over.
