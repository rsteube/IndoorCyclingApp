Indoor Bike 2.12

Run practice from the bike: pedal your trainer and a companion game runs a trail runner along a race course.

Added
- **Run practice in a companion game.** A game can make a ride a run: a trail runner on its course, driven by pedalling your ordinary smart trainer. It is for learning a race course and for aerobic cross-training before it, with no treadmill and no foot pod. Col de Berg is the first game to offer it, with the K110 Innsbruck course.
- **What the phone shows during a run.** Pace in min/km instead of speed, and the course's real grade. There are no shift paddles and no gear card: the game gives the runner one gear, and every shifter, Zwift Click included, is ignored until the next ride.
- **What the trainer feels.** The load the game sets for the runner, and nothing of a bicycle's air or momentum, so a steep wall stays turnable on the one gear.
- **What is saved.** The run is saved and exported as a bike ride on purpose, so an invented pace never lands in your running records or your Strava bests. Its summary shows the distance and average pace instead of a distance estimated from power.

Notes
- A bike ride after a run puts you back on your own bike.
- Runs need a game that offers them. The iPhone and Sailfish OS apps speak the new protocol but ride such a game as a bike for now.
- Companion games: this is Companion API 0.14. The new `course.sport` and `world.courseGradient` fields and the `run` capability are described in the Companion API documentation. All of it is optional, and a game that sends none of it rides exactly as before.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices, connections and settings carry over.
