Indoor Bike 2.0.2

The app answers when you touch it.

Changed
- **Everything you touch now moves on a spring.** A press, a filter chip, a card, a row in the pairing list: all of them answer with physics rather than a fixed-duration curve. A finger expects a physical answer and a tween has none, which is why the old interface felt correct and slightly dead at the same time.

- **Numbers count up to what they are.** A finished ride's figures and a workout's stats land rather than appearing, and lists reveal in sequence instead of all at once - capped, so a long list never makes you wait to read it.

- **The actions that confirm something give a haptic tick.** Adding a workout, pairing a device, finishing a ride: the ones where you want to know it took, and not the ones you do fifty times a minute.

- **Reduced motion is honoured throughout.** Turn it on in Android's accessibility settings and everything simply arrives, with nothing left half-revealed. The same is true of the website, which now moves the same way and stops when your browser asks it to.

Fixed
- **The routes list on a repository's community page laid out on top of itself.** Every route drew its shape at the full width of its card and squeezed the name, place and figures into a column a few pixels wide, which then spilled outside the card. A repository publishing eighteen routes made a page of it. This was a website bug, so it is already fixed for everyone whether or not you update.

Notes
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices and settings carry over.
- No functional changes to riding: the trainer, the routes and the recording behave exactly as they did in 2.0.1.
