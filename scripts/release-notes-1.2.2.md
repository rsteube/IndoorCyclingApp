Indoor Bike 1.2.2

Changed
- **Ride in any orientation**: the ride screen is no longer locked to landscape - it follows your device's rotation, so you can train in portrait or landscape. The HUD picks its layout from the actual screen space: a vertical stack in portrait (big power on top, cadence and heart rate beneath), a compact three-across on a landscape phone, and the full three-across with the live stats strip on tablets and foldables.

Fixed
- **Heart rate and the other metrics no longer get cut off or overlap**: the HUD used to switch into the tablet layout on short screens and clip the readouts. Sizes now scale to the available height so nothing is hidden by accident, and the portrait header reflows to two rows instead of squashing the workout title and status into a sliver.
