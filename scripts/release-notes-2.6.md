Indoor Bike 2.6

The pictures a companion sends during a ride can now be shared.

Added
- **Share a picture from a ride.** Open a ride's screenshots, swipe to the one you want, and there is a share button beside the close button. It goes to whatever your phone shares with: a chat, your photo roll, a file manager, a printer. One picture at a time, the one on screen, and only when you ask for it.

Notes
- This changes nothing about what the app does on its own. Screenshots are still kept on the phone with their ride, are not part of an exported file, and are not uploaded or synced anywhere. Sharing one is your decision, taken one picture at a time.
- Screenshots come from a companion app that answers the phone's request for them, so this is only visible on rides that have some.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices and settings carry over.

Install
- Add this repo to [ObtainX](https://github.com/bikram-agarwal/ObtainX) for automatic updates, or grab the APK below.

Signed with the project's release key; `versionCode 22`.
