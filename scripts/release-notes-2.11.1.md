Indoor Bike 2.11.1

A route ride started part way along now counts from where it started.

Changed
- **Distances count from where you started.** Start a long route at its 600 km mark and the ride screen now says 0 km ridden, not 600. The strip under the elevation profile shows Speed, Ridden, To go and Climbed, with Ridden and Climbed counted from your start and the climbing measured against the stretch ahead of you rather than the whole road.

Fixed
- **The road was drawn as ridden from the trailhead** on the ride summary, in past rides and on the share image when the ride had started part way along. The elevation profile now shades only the stretch you rode, and both it and the route shape mark where the ride began as well as where it ended. The summary no longer says the part before your start was ridden last time, since you may have skipped it on purpose.

Notes
- On the iPhone and Sailfish OS apps nothing changes: routes there start from the beginning or from where you stopped.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices, connections and settings carry over.
