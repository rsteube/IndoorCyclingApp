Indoor Bike 2.8.1

One fix: a flatter road at speed could turn the pedals into a wall.

Fixed
- **Down a fast descent in the hardest gear, a road easing off made the pedals go heavy at once.** At 70 km/h on the gravel 1x, a descent flattening from steep to about -2 % asked the trainer for the equivalent of a 20 % climb, and it took several seconds to let go. On a bike your legs are not connected to a wheel they cannot keep up with: the 40x10 needs 132 rpm to reach the wheel at 70 km/h, and a flatter road can only slow the bike down. Now, while your legs are turning slower than the wheel, a road getting harder reaches you through the bike's momentum rather than at the pedals: the bike slows, and the resistance comes back as your legs catch up with it. Legs turning with the wheel meet a climb exactly as before, and so does a rider changing down as a climb starts. This works in route rides and companion games alike, and games need no change.

Notes
- How your cadence maps onto the road depends on the gear your own bike sits in on the trainer, so the app measures it from the first stretch of steady riding in each ride, about twenty seconds after pulling away. Until then nothing is held back, and without a cadence reading the ride behaves exactly as in 2.8.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices, connections and settings carry over.
