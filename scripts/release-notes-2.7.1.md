Indoor Bike 2.7.1

One fix: the share sheet could put its own buttons off the bottom of the screen.

Fixed
- **Save and Share could be out of reach when sharing a ride.** The card preview is square, so on a phone it is as tall as the sheet is wide, and with the new picture chooser underneath it the sheet wanted more room than the screen had. What did not fit was simply cut off, and what did not fit was the two buttons the sheet exists for. The sheet scrolls now, the buttons stay where they are, the preview is capped at about half the screen so most phones need no scrolling at all, and a sheet tall enough to reach the top of the screen no longer starts underneath the clock.

Notes
- Everything in 2.7 is unchanged: the mid-ride crash fix, companion mode opening when you tap it, the gear on the companion ride screen, and a ride's own screenshots on the card you share.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices and settings carry over.
