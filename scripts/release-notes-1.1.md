Indoor Bike 1.1

New
- Export past rides as **FIT** or **TCX** files and share them to Strava, Garmin Connect, intervals.icu, TrainingPeaks and more - straight from a ride's overview, fully offline.
- **Tablet & large-screen UI**: a side navigation rail, a multi-column workout list, and a redesigned ride HUD with bigger numbers and a live stats strip (elapsed, average power, interval progress).

Improved
- **Backup & sync safety**: the app now detects when local data looks accidentally reset (a reinstall or wipe) and pauses syncing instead of overwriting your backup, with one-tap **Restore from backup** / keep-local options. Previous backup files are also kept as a recoverable `.prev` copy.

Install
- Add this repo to [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) for automatic updates, or grab the APK below.

Signed with the project's release key; `versionCode 2`.
