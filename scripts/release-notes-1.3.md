Indoor Bike 1.3

Added
- **End & save a paused ride**: when a ride is paused - either you paused it, or it auto-paused because you stopped pedaling - you can now tap "End & save" to finish and keep it in your history, instead of pedaling all the way to the end. A short confirmation makes clear the ride so far is saved and can't be resumed.
- **Redesigned home screen**: the "Ride" screen leads with an "Up next" hero that proposes one workout to do next and starts it in a single tap when the trainer is connected, plus a "Quick picks" shelf of your starred and most-ridden workouts.
- **Search and duration filters**: find a workout by name or tag, and filter the library by length (up to 30 min, 30-60, 60+) alongside Favourites and tag filters.
- **Favourite from the list**: star or unstar a workout straight from the library row.
- **Ride again from history**: each past session has a "Ride again" button that jumps back to that workout.
- **Trainer status chip and QR scan in the top bar**: a single status chip opens pairing, and a dedicated button scans a workout QR code from the web editor.

Changed
- **"Get workouts" reorganised**: scanning from the web editor is the headline action; Import file and Paste URL are secondary, and imported-workout management is tucked behind an expandable row.
- **Cleaner ride header**: the landscape header drops the workout title so the live state and clock have room, and an auto-paused ride reads "AUTO-PAUSED · PEDAL TO RESUME".

Fixed
- **Shared ride card chart**: a ride that ended early now plots its power curve on the planned time axis so it lines up with the workout's zone bars.
- **Filter no longer strands the list**: if the active filter disappears (unstarring your last favourite, or deleting a workout), the list falls back to "All".
