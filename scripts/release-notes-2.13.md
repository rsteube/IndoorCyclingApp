Indoor Bike 2.13

Run practice in ERG: the trainer holds your run at a power target, and the paddles move it.

Added
- **Run practice in ERG.** With a companion game that offers it, a run is now ridden at a power target rather than as a load. The game sets the opening watts, the endurance pace it plans the run at, and the trainer holds them however fast you pedal.
- **Move the target with the paddles.** The **Lower** and **Raise** paddles, Zwift Click and the keyboard move the target 5 W at a time, and the game's own buttons do the same. The target is shown where the gear would be, and it is kept through pauses, so a break does not undo what you set.
- **Cadence for runners.** During a run the ride screen shows your cadence large, with a quiet 80-100 rpm band: the range a runner training on a bike should hold, 160 to 200 steps a minute. It is a hint, not an alarm, and nothing enforces it.

Notes
- The usual ERG safety applies: stop pedalling for two seconds and the trainer lets go; pedal again and the target comes back over three seconds.
- Runs are still saved as bike rides, so they stay out of your running records.
- A game that does not offer ERG runs rides them as in 2.12.
- Companion games: this is Companion API 0.15. The new `runPower` capability, a run's power target and its 5 W steps are described in the Companion API documentation. All of it is optional.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices, connections and settings carry over.
