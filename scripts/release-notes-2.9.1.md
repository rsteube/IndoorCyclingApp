Indoor Bike 2.9.1

Rides on FitTrackee keep their figures where you can choose who sees them.

Changed
- **A ride's figures on FitTrackee go in its description, not its private notes.** The line saying the ride was recorded by Indoor Bike, with its average and normalised power, cadence, heart rate, TSS and kJ, now goes in the workout's description. That follows the visibility you set for the workout, like its duration and distance, and the private notes are left for you to write in. Thanks to the FitTrackee rider who suggested it.

Notes
- Rides already on FitTrackee keep their figures in their notes.
- Under the hood, the Bluetooth layer and the power and heart-rate zone tables moved so the Sailfish OS version can share them. Nothing about how the Android app works is meant to have changed.
- Nothing to redo. Your workouts, history, paired devices, connections and settings carry over.
