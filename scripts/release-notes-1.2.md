Indoor Bike 1.2

New
- **Report a problem**: a Settings entry (and a Report link on in-app error banners) drafts an email to the developer with recent diagnostic logs plus your device model, Android version, and app version. No personal data (no name, email, body metrics, or location) is gathered, and nothing is sent until you press send.
- **"Connected" notification**: a brief in-app banner confirms when your trainer, heart-rate monitor, or controller connects, including silent reconnects after a device wakes from sleep.

Improved
- **Much more persistent device connection**: paired trainers, heart-rate monitors, and controllers keep trying to reconnect instead of giving up after one scan. Open the app before waking your trainer and it connects once the trainer starts advertising; a mid-ride drop recovers on its own. Scanning is limited to when the app is in use or a ride is active to spare the battery, and an already-paired device is no longer asked to pair again.

Fixed
- **ERG mode on strict trainers (e.g. Zwift Hub)**: the app now enables FTMS Control Point indications before sending target power, which these trainers require, so ERG mode holds the target watts instead of silently doing nothing. When ERG control does fail, a clear banner explains why.
