New: run practice in ERG. With a companion game that offers it, your run is held at a power target: the game sets the opening watts, and the + and - paddles, Zwift Click or the keyboard move it 5 W at a time. Cadence is shown large with a quiet 80-100 rpm band, the range a runner training on a bike should hold. The target is kept through pauses. Col de Berg is the first game to offer it.
