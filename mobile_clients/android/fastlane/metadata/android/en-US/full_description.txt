Indoor Bike connects to your smart trainer over standard BLE FTMS, runs structured power-zone workouts in ERG mode, and saves rides on your phone as files. No accounts, no subscriptions, no cloud required.

Features:

* BLE smart-trainer control — Wahoo Kickr Core is the first-class target; any FTMS-compatible trainer should work. Optional pairing for a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor and a Zwift Click controller.

* ERG mode — the app drives the trainer to each interval's target wattage automatically. You hold cadence, it holds watts.

* Plain-text JSON workouts — readable, hand-editable, shareable. Ships with a starter pack; import your own via file pick, share-sheet, QR code from the browser editor, or by URL.

* Local progression — streaks, rider levels, badges, week-grouped ride history, and time-in-zone, all computed on-device from saved rides.

* Optional folder-based backup & cross-device sync. Pick any folder your phone can reach (Drive, Dropbox, Nextcloud, Syncthing, OneDrive, …) and the app writes a small JSON slice there. Other devices pointed at the same folder share rides, library, and settings. No accounts, no central server.

* FIT export and optional Intervals.icu sync. Strict opt-in — manual upload from the post-ride screen, or auto-upload after a ride. The app makes zero outbound network calls until you configure credentials.

* F-Droid posture — no Google Play Services, no analytics, no crash reporters, no proprietary blobs. Bundled fonts ship as OFL TTFs.

The companion website at indoorbike.app hosts a browser-based workout editor that produces QR codes the in-app scanner imports locally — no backend round-trip.
