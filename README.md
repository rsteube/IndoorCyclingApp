# Indoor Cycling App

A simple, local-first, open-source indoor cycling app for Android that connects to your smart trainer (Wahoo Kickr Core, etc.) via standard BLE FTMS. It runs structured workouts using plain JSON files and controls your trainer in ERG mode based on your FTP.

## Features

- **No Accounts, No Subscriptions:** Everything runs completely offline on your device by default. 
- **ERG Mode Control:** Your smart trainer is automatically controlled to hit target power zones.
- **JSON Workouts:** Create, edit, and share workouts easily in a readable text format.
- **Privacy First:** Built for F-Droid without any Google Play Services or tracking SDKs.
- **Zwift Click Support:** Adjust workout intensity up/down mid-ride using a Bluetooth controller.
